It's rare we get to jam in 2 bike camping trips in a year so an even rarer treat when we get 3. This one came fresh on the heels of the Marin Headlands ride and was essentially a re-do of the last Santa Ynez ride we attempted in May of '24. In that trip, we had no idea if Lower Camuesa Rd would continue with mild issue or impassible ruts. Doing the full Santa Ynez traverse was something Paco was dying to be able to say he'd done so he'd been scouting some of that area on day rides in the time between and confirmed it was not only doable but probably less of a problem than the route we thought was the "safe" bet. 

This time we brought Steve along and started from Paco's house, quick pop over for bagels and coffee, then via bus up to the foot of where the 33 starts entering the Los Padres NF.
